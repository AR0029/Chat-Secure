# Chat Secure - Privacy-First Messaging Application

**Chat Secure** is a privacy-first messaging app designed to offer secure, anonymous, and decentralized communication. Built with **end-to-end encryption** and a strong focus on user privacy, **Chat Secure** enables users to communicate securely without needing phone numbers, emails, or personal data. Whether you are chatting one-on-one or in groups, **Chat Secure** ensures your conversations remain private, anonymous, and completely under your control.

## Features

### 🔐 **End-to-End Encryption**
All messages, calls, and media shared within **Chat Secure** are protected by **AES-256** end-to-end encryption. This guarantees that only the sender and the intended recipient can access the content, ensuring that third parties, including the app's developers, cannot intercept or read your messages.

### 🔑 **No Phone Numbers or Emails Required**
Unlike traditional messaging apps, **Chat Secure** does not require users to provide any **personal information**. There are no phone numbers, emails, or account registrations needed. Users communicate through a **unique, anonymous username** that does not link to any real-world identity, ensuring complete **anonymity**.

### 🌍 **Decentralized Network**
**Chat Secure** uses a **decentralized architecture**, meaning there is no central server storing or tracking messages. By leveraging technologies like **peer-to-peer (P2P)** communication, messages are routed through a **distributed network** (similar to **Session**'s use of the Oxen Network), ensuring that your conversations are never stored on any centralized platform.

### 🕳 **Self-Destructing Messages**
Send messages that automatically **self-destruct** after they are read or after a specified timer. This feature ensures that your communication is **short-lived** and can’t be retrieved once it expires, reducing the risk of unwanted data exposure.

### 🗣 **Group Chats**
Create and manage **secure group chats** with end-to-end encryption. Group admins control who can join and leave, providing full control over membership and ensuring that group conversations remain private and secure.

### 📤 **Secure File Sharing**
Share files securely with end-to-end encryption, just like your messages. Whether you're sending images, documents, videos, or other media, **Chat Secure** ensures that your files are protected from eavesdropping or interception.

### 🚫 **No Metadata Collection**
**Chat Secure** avoids collecting any **metadata**, such as **IP addresses**, timestamps, or message logs. This means that no one can track your activity or profile you based on your communication habits.

### 📱 **Cross-Platform Support**
**Chat Secure** is available on **Android**, **iOS**, and **Desktop** platforms (via a web app or standalone apps). This ensures that you can access your secure conversations across all your devices while maintaining the highest levels of privacy.

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Anonymity-Focused Usernames**
Instead of phone numbers or emails, users create an **anonymous username** for chatting. This helps ensure that your conversations are **detached from your real-world identity** while still allowing easy communication with others.

### 🛠 **Customizable Notification Settings**
Take control of your notifications with **Chat Secure**. You can set up alerts for individual chats or globally, ensuring that you’re notified only when necessary while preserving your privacy.

### 🌗 **Dark & Light Modes**
Switch between **dark mode** and **light mode** depending on your preference, allowing you to customize your experience for both comfort and visual appeal.

### 💾 **Local Chat Backups**
**Chat Secure** allows users to back up their messages locally on their devices, ensuring that no data is stored on external servers unless you explicitly back it up. Your messages and media are protected with **AES-256 encryption** while stored on your device.

### 🔒 **Push Notifications**
Receive real-time notifications through **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**, ensuring you stay updated with new messages while preserving the security and privacy of your communication.

### 🌐 **No Cloud Storage**
No data is stored in the cloud by default. This means that **Chat Secure** never collects or uploads your messages, files, or any other personal data unless you decide to back up your content locally. This aligns with the app’s commitment to **decentralization** and **user control** over their data.

### 🛡 **Complete Control Over Your Data**
With **Chat Secure**, **you are in control of your data**. There’s no tracking, no collection of personal information, and no data that can be used to identify you. You decide how long your messages last and whether you want to back them up.

By using **end-to-end encryption**, **decentralized communication**, and providing **no need for personal information**, **Chat Secure** empowers you to control your privacy without compromising on functionality or ease of use.
